How this works & its limits
  • The list: this isn't a contest. By default, hams are listed by when they were last heard, grouped into Heard today, This week and Earlier, like a net roster. Switch to A–Z, or click a column header to sort by that column. Active days are distinct (UTC) days on which a ham made a logged QSO or was heard on the air.
  • Logged QSOs come from the public Parks on the Air and Summits on the Air logs for activations of Hawaii parks and summits. These are real contacts. SOTA doesn't report modes, so SOTA QSOs only appear under "All".
  • POTA hunter QSOs are contacts with park activators anywhere in the world, as logged by the activator. Hunters don't need to upload anything. POTA's public profile shows only a ham's 25 most recent hunter QSOs, and only for hams with a POTA account. So this covers about 120 Hawaii hams, history starts at each one's latest 25 contacts, and new contacts are captured each day going forward. The station's location isn't recorded, so residents are assumed to be hunting from home.
  • Net check-ins come from the public net logs on 40meter.net. These include the HF nets (Aloha, Hi-Noon and Afternoon nets on 7.090 MHz, plus Bandhopper, Mesh, ARES, 10-10 and HF hospital nets) and the VHF/UHF and digital nets (EARC Evening Net, BIARC, PRRC, East Hawaii ARES, the Hawaii DMR Net on TG 311551, C4FM and simplex nets). Each check-in counts as an active day for that station: voice for most nets, data for Winlink nets. Net control is shown separately. 40meter.net only keeps recent nets, so net data starts in late October / November 2026.
  • Times heard comes from the Reverse Beacon Network (mostly CW, plus some FT8/RTTY) and PSK Reporter (FT8, FT4, JS8 and other data modes, counted when the sending station's grid is in Hawaii). These count automatic decodes of a station's signal, not QSOs. Beacons and WSPR are excluded. PSK Reporter data starts November 1, 2026.
  • Residents are callsigns with an active FCC license and a Hawaii mailing address. Island is taken from the ZIP code. Non-residents are either operating from Hawaii (a POTA/SOTA activation here, or signing /KH6) with a license registered elsewhere, or checking into a Hawaii net with a license registered elsewhere. Unverified means heard with a Hawaii prefix but not found in the active FCC database. These may be expired licenses or callsigns the receivers decoded incorrectly.
  • Not covered: other voice activity (repeaters, VHF/UHF nets, SSB ragchews), personal logs (LoTW, QRZ, Club Log) and anyone not using the modes above. Voice activity is heavily undercounted, so treat this as a lower bound on how many hams are active.
  • Days are UTC days, as in ham radio logging. A new day starts at 2 pm Hawaii time, so afternoon and evening activity counts toward the next UTC date. Periods like "Last week" are counted in UTC days, and today's UTC day is still in progress.
  • A Hawaii resident is counted only when operating in Hawaii. Activations by Hawaii residents on the mainland are not included.

Achievement rules (version 1.0) · achievements start December 1, 2026

The Hawaii Amateur Radio Activity Achievements recognize hams who are active on the air in Hawaii. Achievements are earned automatically from the same public data as the list of active hams. There's nothing to apply for. Click a callsign to see a ham's achievements and download certificates. Each certificate has a verification link.

How activity is counted

  • Start: activity counts from December 1, 2026, 00:00 UTC. Earlier activity doesn't count toward achievements.
  • Days are UTC days, as in ham radio logging. A new day starts at 2 pm Hawaii time.
  • Active day: a day with at least one net check-in, POTA or SOTA QSO, POTA hunter QSO logged by an activator, or spot on the Reverse Beacon Network or PSK Reporter.
  • All Modes: a month qualifies when it has at least one active day each on voice, CW and data. The days don't need to be the same, and the months don't need to be consecutive. SOTA QSOs don't count here, because SOTA doesn't report the mode.
  • Outages: if data collection was down on a given day (no activity recorded from anyone), that day doesn't break a Consecutive Days run.
  • Who can earn achievements: Hawaii residents (active FCC license with a Hawaii address) and non-residents operating from or checking into nets in Hawaii. Unverified callsigns, which may be misread by the spotting networks, are excluded.

Good to know

  • Only visible activity counts. Achievements are based on publicly available activity data. Contacts that don't appear in these sources can't be counted.
  • Beta: the program is in a beta phase. Achievements, levels and rules may still change, and achievements may be corrected if they were based on wrong data, for example a misread callsign.
  • No guarantee: this is a volunteer hobby project. It may be changed, paused or ended at any time. Certificates you've downloaded are yours to keep.
  • Unofficial: these are unofficial achievements with no monetary value. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by POTA, SOTA, the Reverse Beacon Network, PSK Reporter, 40meter.net, the FCC or the ARRL.
  • Opt-out and name display: a self-service form for leaving the list and the achievements, or for hiding your name on certificates, is coming soon. Requests are applied automatically at the next update.
